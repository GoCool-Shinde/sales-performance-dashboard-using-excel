# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard – Excel

An interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel** to analyze sales, profit, customer count, product performance, and monthly sales trends.

The dashboard converts raw sales data into an interactive visual report that helps users understand business performance across different **categories, products, customers, states, and years**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Businesses often have large amounts of transactional sales data, but analyzing raw data directly can make it difficult to identify important trends and performance patterns.

The objective of this project was to transform raw sales data into an **interactive Excel dashboard** that provides a consolidated view of sales and profitability and allows users to explore the data using filters.

The dashboard can help management answer questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which categories are performing well?
- How has profit changed over time?
- How are sales distributed across different states?
- How many customers are active each year?
- Which customers contribute significantly to overall profit?
- How do sales change throughout the year?

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The business has a large sales dataset containing information about products, categories, customers, locations, sales, profit, and dates.

Analyzing this information manually from raw transactional records can be time-consuming and makes it difficult to quickly identify:

- Top-performing products
- Sales and profit trends
- Customer contribution
- Regional performance
- Monthly sales patterns
- Changes in performance across different years

Therefore, an interactive dashboard was required to provide management with a **single view of important business KPIs and trends**.

---

## 💡 Solution

I developed an interactive **Sales Dashboard in Excel** that transforms the raw sales data into meaningful visual insights.

The dashboard includes:

- Interactive category filters
- Year filters
- Sales by product/category
- Profit trend analysis
- Sales by state
- Customer count by year
- Top 5 customers by profit
- Monthly sales trend

Users can apply filters to dynamically analyze the dashboard based on different categories and years.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by Category

Displays sales performance across different products/categories.

This helps identify the products contributing the most to overall sales.

### 2. Profit Gained Over Time

Shows how profit changes across different years for:

- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

This allows users to compare profitability trends between categories.

### 3. Sales by State

Provides a geographical view of sales performance across states.

This helps identify regions with stronger sales performance and potential areas for improvement.

### 4. Customer Count

Displays the number of customers across different years.

This helps track changes in the customer base over time.

### 5. Top 5 Customers Making Profit

Highlights the top customers based on profit contribution.

This helps identify high-value customers who have a significant impact on overall profitability.

### 6. Monthly Sales

Shows sales performance across the months of the year.

This helps identify seasonal patterns, high-performing months, and periods with relatively lower sales.

---

## 🔎 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- **Phones** are the highest-selling product in the displayed product-level sales analysis.
- **Chairs and Storage** are also among the stronger contributors to sales.
- **Technology** shows a strong upward profit trend across the analyzed years.
- **Office Supplies** also demonstrates positive profit growth over time.
- Customer count varies across years, allowing customer-base growth to be monitored.
- Sales fluctuate significantly across months, indicating potential seasonal patterns.
- A small group of top customers contributes a meaningful share of total profit.

> Note: These insights are based on the dataset used for this portfolio project and are intended to demonstrate analytical capabilities.
